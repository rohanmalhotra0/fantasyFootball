## 1. TL;DR — the strategy this year, in this scoring

**The market is drafting a different league than yours.** ADP comes from
4-pt-TD leagues; you're in a 6-pt-TD league. That one rule moves top QBs up
~15–20 ranks and creates the three biggest edges on the board:

1. **WR early, not RB.** The instinct of "3 RB first picks" is a losing play
   this year in this scoring: model has 5 WRs in the top-5 non-RB values,
   and the RBs going in the late first (Jeanty ADP 12, Hampton ADP 19,
   K. Walker ADP 21) are model fades — Hampton is model rank **93** vs ADP 19.
   Rounds 1–2 should be WR/elite-RB only (Bijan, Gibbs, CMC, Achane are the
   only RBs worth round-1 price).
2. **Elite TE is the cheapest superstar on the board.** Trey McBride is the
   #6 overall value (VORP 110) but goes at ADP 31 — a full two rounds late.
   Fannin (ADP 69, model #15) and Warren (ADP 57, model #17) mean TE value
   persists into R5–6 if you miss McBride. You should leave every draft with
   one of these three.
3. **QB matters here — but the market doesn't know it.** With 6-pt TDs:
   Burrow is the #14 overall value (ADP 50 → ~round 5!), Goff #24 (ADP 100!),
   Mayfield #33 (ADP 121), Bo Nix / Caleb Williams top-60 values at ADP 98–112.
   You do NOT need to reach: take QB1 in R4–5, or wait and still get a
   top-tier one in R9–10. Never draft a 2nd QB before the last rounds.
4. **RB volume comes late.** After the big four, target the value cluster:
   TreVeyon Henderson (ADP 57, model #18!), Chase Brown, Henry, then
   RJ Harvey (ADP 93, model #35). Plan on 3–4 RBs in rounds 4–8.

**Slot preference: pick EARLY.** Slots 1–4 project ~70–80 more VORP than
mid slots because the four true difference-makers (Puka, Bijan, Chase, JSN)
are gone by pick 5, while everything the mid slots get is also available on
the way back. Ranking: **1 ≈ 2 ≈ 3 > 4 > 8 > 10 > 7 > 5 ≈ 6 > 9.**

## 6. Things you didn't ask about but need

- **Bye weeks are NOT in this data** (the ADP seed lacks them). Excel Claude: add a BYE column from any 2026 bye table and flag if QB+TE or WR1+WR2 share a bye. Don't let byes veto a value pick before round 8, only break ties.
- **Rookies have no model projection** (no NFL history — the model is honest about it). Jeanty (ADP 12), Hampton (19), Skattebo (37) show as fades partly for this reason; their "model rank" leans on ADP-neighborhood value. Treat rookie RBs in R1–3 as market-priced lottery tickets the model refuses to endorse — that's a feature.
- **Risk flags** (⚠️ in CSV): projection leans on an injury/holdout-shortened prior season (Burrow ⚠️ — his profile is elite but the sample is thin; that's WHY he's cheap). Take the discount knowingly.
- **Survival rule while drafting**: if a target's `ADP ≤ your next pick − 6`, he won't make it back — take him now or say goodbye. That's the P(survival) math the draft room uses.
- **In-draft tool**: the DraftEngine draft room (repo: `make dev`) does all of this live — recommendations with reasons, voice pick logging, opponent tracking — set League Settings to 10 teams/custom/pass_td=6 first and everything above recomputes automatically.
- **ADP caveat**: market data is a 12-team full-PPR sample (FFC; the 2025-season endpoint is broken server-side, and 10-team samples are thin). In a 10-team room everything falls ~2–4 picks later than listed ADP — values get MORE available, fades get less excusable.

## 7. For Excel Claude — implementation guide

**Data**: `board_2026_10team_ppr_6ptQB.csv` (same folder). 120 rows. Columns:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `overall_rank` | Model rank under THIS league's settings (sort key) |
| `name`, `position`, `team` | Identity (team may be blank for a few) |
| `projected_points` | 2026 season points, full PPR + 6-pt pass TD |
| `vorp` | `projected_points − replacement_points` at that position |
| `adp`, `adp_rank` | Market draft position (12-team PPR sample) |
| `value_gap` | `adp_rank − model rank` (positive = value, negative = fade) |
| `tier` | Gap-based tier within position (1 = elite) |
| `risk_flag` | TRUE = projection built on a short prior season |

**Constants (10-team, this roster):** replacement level = QB11, RB23,
WR29, TE11 (points of the Nth player at that position). If you rebuild VORP:
`vorp = projected_points − VLOOKUP(position, replacement_table, 2)` where
replacement_table holds the projected points of QB#11, RB#23, WR#29, TE#11
from the sorted board.

**Snake pick formula** (10 teams, slot S, round R):
`=IF(ISODD(R), (R-1)*10 + S, R*10 - S + 1)` — build a 10×15 grid with this.

**Suggested sheets:**
1. `Board` — raw CSV, sorted by `overall_rank`. Conditional formatting:
   green scale on `value_gap` > 0, red < −15; ⚠️ icon where `risk_flag`;
   color band by `tier` within position.
2. `Slots` — the 10×15 snake grid (formula above); for each of MY picks,
   `INDEX/MATCH` the best available `overall_rank` whose `adp_rank ≥ overall − 2`
   (the availability heuristic used in section 5).
3. `Cheat` — pivot: top 8 by `vorp` per position with `adp` column, plus
   the Values and Fades lists from section 3 verbatim.
4. `Tiers` — `COUNTIFS` per position×tier + the cliff table from section 4.

**Rules to encode, in priority order** (this is the whole strategy as an
algorithm): 1) R1–2 take best available from {WR T1–T3, RB T1–T2} by vorp;
2) if McBride available and my next pick > ADP 31 → take him now;
3) QB: take Burrow/Goff/Lamar/Mayfield at the LAST pick before their ADP,
   never two QBs before R12; 4) RB volume R4–8 from the value list;
5) never K/DST before R14; 6) tie-break equal vorp by (lower adp_rank
   first if `adp ≤ next_pick − 6`, else higher tier, else bye conflict).

**What Excel Claude still needs from elsewhere**: 2026 bye weeks (add
column), any injury news after 2026-07-30 (this file is a snapshot —
the model knows nothing after that date), and your league's exact bench
size if it differs from 6.
